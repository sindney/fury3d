# sky-atmosphere

## Purpose

Precomputed-LUT sky atmosphere rendering (a GL 3.3 port of the Unreal Engine
SkyAtmosphere technique, EGSR 2020): the `SkyAtmosphere` component, LUT
generation via fragment-shader passes, the pipeline sky pass, aerial
perspective for opaque geometry, sun/moon discs, a 2D cloud layer, and
time-of-day driving the scene's dominant directional light as the sun.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The engine SHALL support 3D textures as render targets

`TextureType` SHALL gain `TEXTURE_3D`; `Texture::CreateEmpty` SHALL allocate
3D textures via `glTexStorage3D`; `Pass` SHALL be able to attach a single
slice of a 3D texture as its color target (`glFramebufferTexture3D`),
mirroring the existing `SetArrayTextureLayer` precedent; `RenderUtil` SHALL
provide a 1x1x1 dummy 3D texture alongside the existing dummy 2D/cube/array
textures.

#### Scenario: Create and render into a 3D texture

- **WHEN** code creates a `Texture` with type `TEXTURE_3D`, format `rgba16f`, depth 32, attaches slice 5 to a `Pass`, and draws a fullscreen quad
- **THEN** slice 5 of the 3D texture contains the drawn output
- **AND** no GL error is raised

#### Scenario: Dummy 3D texture binds as a safe fallback

- **WHEN** a shader declaring a `sampler3D` is drawn without a real 3D texture bound
- **THEN** the dummy 3D texture is bound instead and the draw completes without a sampler-target-mismatch failure

### Requirement: The shader compiler SHALL support a local `#include` directive

`Shader::Compile` SHALL resolve lines of the form `#include "Name.glsl"`
against the including file's directory (recursively, with cycle/depth guard)
before compilation, so the five atmosphere shaders can share one common-math
file without hand-maintained copies. Only quoted relative includes are
required; angle-bracket and absolute includes are out of scope.

#### Scenario: Shared atmosphere math compiled from one file

- **WHEN** two atmosphere shaders both contain `#include "AtmosphereCommon.glsl"` and the common file defines a shared function
- **THEN** both shaders compile and link successfully
- **AND** editing only the common file changes both shaders' behavior on next pipeline load

#### Scenario: Include cycle fails with a clear error

- **WHEN** shader A includes B and B includes A
- **THEN** compilation fails with a logged error naming the cycle, not a hang or crash

### Requirement: SkyAtmosphere SHALL be a serializable scene component holding atmosphere and time-of-day parameters

`SkyAtmosphere` SHALL be a `Component` registered in
`SceneNode::ComponentRegistry` (name `SkyAtmosphere`) with: planet radius,
atmosphere thickness, Rayleigh/Mie/ozone coefficients, Mie phase g, ground
albedo, sun angular diameter, cloud settings (enable, coverage, altitude,
scale, wind speed), time-of-day settings (time in hours [0,24), day length in
real minutes, auto-advance flag, sun-from-TOD flag), and a sun-light node
name. It SHALL implement `Load`/`Save`/`Clone` per the component contract.
Scenes without the component SHALL load unchanged.

#### Scenario: Round-trip a sky component

- **WHEN** a scene containing a configured `SkyAtmosphere` node is saved and reloaded
- **THEN** all atmosphere, cloud, and time-of-day parameters and the sun-light name are restored exactly

#### Scenario: Legacy scenes load untouched

- **WHEN** a scene saved before this change (e.g. `outdoor_physics.bin`) is loaded
- **THEN** loading succeeds with no sky component present and rendering is pixel-identical to before

### Requirement: Atmosphere LUTs SHALL be generated by fragment-shader passes into float render targets

The component SHALL own a transmittance LUT (256x64 rgba16f), a
multi-scattering LUT (32x32 rgba16f), a sky-view LUT (192x108 rgba16f), and a
camera aerial-perspective volume (3D rgba16f, 32 depth slices, squared slice
distribution, 4 km per slice as in the reference). Transmittance and
multi-scatter SHALL be re-rendered only when atmosphere parameters change;
sky-view and the camera volume SHALL be re-rendered per frame when the sun
direction or camera has changed. No compute shaders SHALL be used; the
reference multi-scatter compute reduction SHALL become a per-fragment loop.

#### Scenario: LUTs regenerate on parameter change

- **WHEN** the user changes the Rayleigh coefficient at runtime
- **THEN** transmittance and multi-scatter LUTs are re-rendered before the next sky draw
- **AND** steady-state frames with unchanged parameters do not re-render those LUTs

#### Scenario: Sky color responds to sun elevation

- **WHEN** time-of-day is set to noon vs sunset and the sky is rendered
- **THEN** the sky-view LUT produces a blue-leaning sky at noon and a red-leaning horizon glow at sunset (verifiable via screenshot pixel sampling)

### Requirement: The PBR pipeline SHALL render the sky into `hdr_composite` after combine and before transparent

`DrawMode` SHALL gain a `SKY` value; the PBR pipeline JSON SHALL declare a
`pass_sky` pass (index between `pass_combine` and `pass_transparent`) whose
output is `hdr_composite`. The sky draw SHALL write only pixels whose gbuffer
depth equals the far value (the existing sky-mask convention), sampling the
sky-view LUT plus sun disc, moon disc, and cloud layer. When no enabled
`SkyAtmosphere` exists in the scene, the pass SHALL no-op (all samplers still
bound to dummies). The LDR Lambert pipeline SHALL remain unchanged.

#### Scenario: Sky appears behind opaque geometry

- **WHEN** a scene with an enabled `SkyAtmosphere` renders in HDR mode
- **THEN** far-depth pixels show the atmosphere gradient and opaque geometry occludes it correctly

#### Scenario: Transparent geometry blends over the sky

- **WHEN** a transparent surface (e.g. the outdoor water plane) is in front of sky pixels
- **THEN** the transparent pass blends over the already-rendered sky color, not over clear color

#### Scenario: Scene without sky renders exactly as before

- **WHEN** a scene with no `SkyAtmosphere` renders in HDR mode
- **THEN** sky-mask pixels keep the previous clear-color result and no GL errors occur

### Requirement: Opaque geometry SHALL receive aerial perspective when a sky is active

The PBR combine shader SHALL, when `u_atmosphere_enabled` is set, apply the
camera aerial-perspective volume to opaque scene pixels: transmittance
attenuates the combined radiance and inscatter is added, using the pixel's
reconstructed view depth. All atmosphere samplers SHALL be bound every draw
(dummies when disabled).

#### Scenario: Distant terrain hazes toward the horizon

- **WHEN** a terrain extends toward the horizon under an active sky
- **THEN** distant pixels shift toward the atmosphere inscatter color with distance, while near pixels are essentially unchanged

#### Scenario: Toggle off restores exact combine output

- **WHEN** `u_atmosphere_enabled` is 0
- **THEN** combine output equals the pre-change shader's output

### Requirement: The sky SHALL render a sun disc and a textured moon with a phase terminator

The sky ray march SHALL add a sun disc (angular diameter from the component,
attenuated by the transmittance LUT) and a moon disc textured with a
generated moon albedo, shaded by a phase terminator derived from the
sun-moon angle. Moon direction SHALL be derived from time-of-day (offset from
the sun); the moon SHALL NOT be a scene light by default.

#### Scenario: Sun disc visible and transmittance-attenuated

- **WHEN** the camera looks at the sun at noon vs near the horizon
- **THEN** the disc is visible in both cases and redder/dimmer near the horizon

#### Scenario: Moon shows correct phase

- **WHEN** time-of-day places sun and moon near opposition vs conjunction
- **THEN** the moon disc renders near-full vs near-new respectively

### Requirement: The sky SHALL render a 2D cloud layer when enabled

When clouds are enabled, the sky pass SHALL raymarch a planar cloud layer at
the configured altitude using generated coverage and detail noise textures,
lit by the sun (single scattering approximation with a short light march),
tinted by time-of-day, animated by wind offset, and composited over the
atmosphere with transmittance. Cloud rendering MAY run at half resolution
composited into the sky pass.

#### Scenario: Clouds respond to coverage and TOD

- **WHEN** coverage is raised from 0.1 to 0.9
- **THEN** the sky visibly fills with cloud
- **AND** at sunset the cloud undersides shift warm

#### Scenario: Clouds off costs nothing

- **WHEN** clouds are disabled
- **THEN** the cloud pass is skipped entirely (no extra target, no march)

### Requirement: When a sky is enabled, the dominant directional light SHALL act as the sun

`SkyAtmosphere` SHALL bind to one directional light by node name (falling
back to the first `DIRECTIONAL` light in the scene). While the sky is enabled
and its sun-from-TOD flag is set, the component SHALL drive that light's
rotation (from the evaluated sun direction), color, and intensity (including
night extinction when the sun is below the horizon); the sky pass SHALL use
the same light's direction as the sun direction, so lighting and sky never
disagree. While the flag is unset, the light stays user-controlled and the
sky follows it instead.

#### Scenario: TOD rotates the sun light

- **WHEN** time-of-day advances from morning to evening with sun-from-TOD set
- **THEN** the bound directional light's direction, color, and intensity track the evaluated sun path (shadow direction visibly follows)

#### Scenario: Sky follows a manually rotated light

- **WHEN** sun-from-TOD is unset and the user rotates the bound light in the editor
- **THEN** the rendered sky's sun position and horizon glow follow the light direction

#### Scenario: Night falls

- **WHEN** TOD evaluates the sun below the horizon
- **THEN** the bound light intensity goes to ~0 and the sky renders the night atmosphere + moon

### Requirement: SkyAtmosphere SHALL have an editor inspector section

`EditorNodeProperties` SHALL render a `SkyAtmosphere` section (registered in
`ComponentRenderTable` and the Add-Component menu) exposing TOD time
slider/day-length/auto-advance, sun-light name, and the atmosphere and cloud
parameters, marking the scene dirty on edit.

#### Scenario: Edit TOD in the inspector

- **WHEN** the user drags the time slider with sun-from-TOD set
- **THEN** the viewport sky, sun position, and light direction update immediately and the scene is marked dirty
